# N1 — Raw BMETS training intensity transfer

The frozen training partition contains 210 patients. The fit reads canonical T1c and aligned raw label 3 at native resolution and includes every eligible foreground voxel once. It uses the stored NIfTI intensity scaling before calculating exact NumPy linear percentiles, the arithmetic mean and population standard deviation in float64. The fit creates one private, verified R2 result per patient and independently verifies each patient and examination against the frozen manifest at reduction. Cases without label 3 count toward coverage and contribute no foreground intensity values.

The [official normalization implementation](https://github.com/MIC-DKFZ/LesionLocator/blob/ef073baaa0dcba8dd036ec5a29f130db18c9d580/lesionlocator/preprocessing/normalization/default_normalization_schemes.py) clips to the 0.5th and 99.5th foreground percentiles before subtracting the mean and dividing by the standard deviation. Those quantiles define a central percentile interval. [nnU-Net normalization documentation](https://github.com/MIC-DKFZ/nnUNet/blob/master/documentation/explanation_normalization.md) defines foreground as annotated non-background, non-ignore classes. Its [reference extractor](https://github.com/MIC-DKFZ/nnUNet/blob/master/nnunetv2/experiment_planning/dataset_fingerprint/fingerprint_extractor.py) samples per training case with replacement, pools the samples and computes unclipped moments. The public checkpoint does not identify its exact underlying sample draw or weighting. The exact pooled BMETS estimator follows the protocol's voxel-weighted definition, so large lesions and exams can have greater influence.

For positive affine correspondence, clipping and both moments transform linearly and the normalized value is invariant. This identifies the four parameters to transfer and leaves the cross-modality correspondence as an empirical hypothesis. All visits use the same fitted parameters. Raw nonzero cropping precedes normalization and resampling. Internal padding remains zero. Label-3 archives and patient-level intensity records remain private.
