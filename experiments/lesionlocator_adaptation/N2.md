# N2 — Independent intensity fidelity

The fit uses Train only. Validation uses 45 frozen patients and 159 examinations, with the same N1 plans digest for all scans. The CT benchmark uses the authors' six public pairs and the original checkpoint normalization. Masks define offline measurement regions only. The work records preserve patient-level distributions and consecutive-visit changes in private R2.

MRI valid-anatomy proxy is nonzero raw T1c. CT uses a threshold above −500 in the released CT values to exclude air and common low padding. The normal-tissue proxy excludes every labelled pathological region. The perilesional proxy is a 5 mm shell of physical voxel-center distance, intersected with valid anatomy and excluding all annotated pathology. The shell has an orthogonal-grid Euclidean distance path and an affine-aware nearest-neighbor path for shear. These are measurable proxies for anatomy and healthy tissue.

Strict clipping fractions use `x < lower` and `x > upper`. Boundary fractions include equal raw values after clipping. Histograms use normalized bins from −8 to 8 in 0.25 steps and two tail bins. Masked-region summaries retain raw and normalized moments and quantiles. Raw panels use the anatomy proxy's within-scan P1–P99 display range. Normalized panels use the same [−3, 3] display range across arms. The original CT and BMETS panels remain private.

CNR uses the signed lesion–shell mean difference over the square root of the average of their variances. Zero denominator is undefined. Contrast retention uses raw-intensity differences before and after clipping to isolate clipping from scale. The group-level contrast distributions preserve sign. Chronological comparisons group signs and absolute differences by adjacent visit order without interpreting scanner or treatment cause.

Across the 144 validation visits with lesion–shell measurements, median clipping-only contrast retention rose from 0.0378% to 99.91%. Median normalized CNR rose from 0.107 (120 defined visits) to 1.589 (144 defined visits). The fitted transform preserved the brighter-lesion sign in all 142 visits with positive raw lesion–shell differences. The 12 source CT exams had both contrast directions and seven positive raw differences. Source median normalized CNR was 0.286 across 11 defined visits. BMETS now retains regional intensity structure while remaining distinct from the sampled CT domain. A single positive affine model cannot account for the full range of source contrast signs.
